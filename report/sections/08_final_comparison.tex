EfficientNetB0 achieves the highest test accuracy, macro precision, and macro recall, but it also has the largest saved model size and the slowest measured epoch time. MobileNetV2 gives a strong accuracy improvement over Model B with a smaller saved model than EfficientNetB0. Model B is far smaller than both pretrained models and has the lowest measured training cost, but its accuracy is lower. Therefore, the preferred model depends on the deployment priority: Model B is most suitable when memory and compute are strict, while EfficientNetB0 is best when accuracy is the main goal and the larger model can be supported.
